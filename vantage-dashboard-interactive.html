Methodology & assumptions
01Loaded hourly rate — $68/hr blended (salary + benefits + overhead), varies by department/level in the underlying model; not a flat company-wide rate.
02Hours-saved calibration — anchored to a task-time benchmark (n=140 sampled tasks) plus a standardized self-report instrument; not inferred from prompt/token volume alone.
03Causal attribution — staggered-rollout design; high-adoption cohorts matched to controls on function and headcount, not raw correlation (see counterfactual chart above).
04Setup cost amortization — straight-line over a 24-month useful-life window.
05Double-count reconciliation — cost-avoidance and outcome-lift value are cross-checked against the same underlying activity; overlap is removed once, not estimated twice.
